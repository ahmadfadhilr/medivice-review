PT Medivice Global CRO (“Medivice”) has prepared this Privacy Policy because we receive information from the people we work with, and because protecting personal data is fundamental to clinical research. This policy sets out, in plain terms, what we collect, why we collect it, and what you can ask us to do with it. Medivice complies with Law No. 27 of 2022 on Personal Data Protection of the Republic of Indonesia (UU PDP).

The information we collect and how we use it

Website visitors

This policy covers information Medivice receives from anyone who visits medivice-cro.com or a landing page connected to it (“Visitor”).

When a Visitor asks for more information about Medivice or our services, we may ask for contact details. These may include name, organisation, job title, telephone number, address and e-mail address. We use this information to do what has been asked of us — to answer an enquiry, send a proposal, or arrange a meeting — and, where the Visitor has agreed, to send occasional invitations to our events and training.

We may also collect information about how you use the site. Cookies may record the pages you open and the links you follow, and your browser sends standard information to every site you visit, such as your IP address, the time of access and the address of the referring page. We use this mainly to produce aggregate statistics about site usage and to improve the site.

Client and partner representatives

This policy also describes how we process personal data about you in your capacity as a representative of a company that is a client, a prospective client, a supplier or a partner of Medivice.

For such representatives we collect contact information — name, address, telephone number, e-mail address, job title, position and employer — either directly from you, from the organisation you represent, or through our partners and suppliers. This normally happens by e-mail, through our website, through professional networks, or at meetings, conferences and training events. We may supplement this with publicly available business information.

We process this personal data in order to:

  • present our services and communicate about our work;
  • provide the clinical research services we have been contracted to deliver;
  • manage contracts, issue proposals and invoices, and receive payment;
  • meet legal obligations, including accounting, tax and regulatory record-keeping;
  • respond to questions, complaints and quality issues;
  • improve the quality of our services and of our client support.

Clinical study participants

If you are taking part in a clinical investigation or clinical study, this policy does not apply to you. Your data is handled under the informed consent form you signed, the clinical investigation plan, ISO 14155 and the approvals granted by the ethics committee and the competent authority. In those studies Medivice normally acts as a Personal Data Processor on the instructions of the study Sponsor, who is the Personal Data Controller. Please contact your investigator site with any question about your study data.

Legal basis for our processing

Under Article 20(2) UU PDP, we process personal data on the following bases:

  • Performance of an agreement, or steps taken at your request before entering into one — responding to enquiries and requests for proposal, and delivering contracted services.
  • Compliance with a legal obligation — accounting and tax records, regulatory submissions, and retention of study documentation.
  • Legitimate interest — maintaining business relationships, securing our website, and improving our services, where this does not override your rights.
  • Consent — analytics cookies, marketing and event communications, and publication of testimonials. Consent may be withdrawn at any time, without affecting processing already carried out.

Who we share your personal data with

We never collect personal data in order to sell it. We may disclose your personal data to our suppliers and partners where they need it to perform their obligations towards us, and to providers of cloud services, since we use cloud solutions to store certain information. Where our clients require it for a project, we may share your contact details with the Sponsor, the investigator site or another study vendor. We may also disclose personal data to regulatory authorities, Notified Bodies, auditors and public authorities where the law or the applicable regulatory framework requires it.

Where a supplier processes personal data on our behalf, this is governed by a written agreement in line with Article 51 UU PDP, and that supplier may not engage a sub-processor without our prior written approval.

Transfers outside Indonesia

Our clients and project teams are located in several countries, and personal data may be transferred outside Indonesia. In accordance with Article 56 UU PDP, we transfer personal data only where the receiving country or recipient provides a level of protection at least equal to that under UU PDP; or, failing that, where adequate and binding safeguards are in place, such as contractual clauses; or, failing both, where you have given your consent. Where the data is also covered by Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR), we apply the equivalent GDPR transfer mechanism as well.

Your rights

Articles 5 to 15 UU PDP give you the following rights.

  • Right to information. You have the right to know who is processing your personal data, on what basis, for what purpose, and for how long.
  • Right of access. You have the right to access your personal data and to obtain a copy of it in a readable format.
  • Right to rectification. If your personal data is inaccurate, incomplete or out of date, you have the right to have it corrected or updated.
  • Right to erasure. You have the right to ask us to delete or destroy your personal data. Unless we have a legal basis to continue processing it, it will be deleted.
  • Right to withdraw consent. Where processing is based on your consent, you may withdraw that consent at any time.
  • Right to object to automated decisions. You have the right to object to decisions taken solely by automated processing, including profiling, that have a legal effect or a significant impact on you.
  • Right to restrict processing. In certain circumstances you have the right to delay or limit our processing of your personal data.
  • Right to data portability. You have the right to receive your personal data and to have it transmitted to another controller, where the systems concerned are compatible.
  • Right to compensation. You have the right to bring a claim and to receive compensation for a violation of your personal data.
  • Right to complain. You have the right to lodge a complaint about our processing with the competent Indonesian supervisory authority. Pending the establishment of the Personal Data Protection Authority (Lembaga Pelindungan Data Pribadi) under Article 58 UU PDP, this function is exercised by the Ministry of Communication and Digital Affairs (Komdigi).

We will respond to a request to access, correct, delete or stop processing your personal data within 3 x 24 hours, as required by UU PDP, unless a legal exception applies. We may ask you to confirm your identity first.

How long we keep personal data

We keep personal data only for as long as it is needed for the purposes described above, or for as long as a legal, contractual or regulatory obligation requires. Study documentation held on behalf of a Sponsor is retained for the period set out in the Sponsor agreement and the applicable regulation. When the period ends, the data is deleted or irreversibly anonymised.

Security

We apply technical and organisational measures proportionate to the risk, in line with Articles 35 and 39 UU PDP, including access on a need-to-know basis, encryption in transit, audit trails on validated systems, confidentiality undertakings and staff training. No transmission over the internet is completely secure, so please do not send confidential or health-related information through the website forms or by unencrypted e-mail.

Personal data breach

If personal data in our care is disclosed or lost, we will notify the affected individuals and the competent authority in writing within 3 x 24 hours of becoming aware of it, as required by Article 46 UU PDP.

Cookies

Cookies are small data files stored on your device, used by most websites today. On their own they do not identify you personally; they recognise the browser being used. We use cookies to keep the site working and secure, and — only if you accept them — to understand how the site is used.

We may also use persistent cookies to recognise you as a returning Visitor. We are careful about what is stored in them; we do not store account numbers or passwords in cookies. You can set your browser to refuse cookies and still browse the site.

Third-party cookies

We may engage third parties to measure and analyse non-identifying usage and volume statistics from visitors to our site. Those parties may set cookies to help track browsing behaviour. Such cookies are not used to link an individual visitor to identifying information, and the data collected on our behalf is used only to give us information about how the site is used.

Third-party websites

Our site contains links to other websites, including professional networks and messaging services. We are not responsible for the content or the privacy practices of those sites. When you follow a link you may be disclosing information about yourself, and it is worth checking the privacy policy of any site you visit.

Children

Our website is not directed at children and we do not knowingly collect personal data of children through it. Where a child’s data is processed in a clinical investigation, it is processed with the consent of a parent or guardian in accordance with Article 25 UU PDP.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The current version and the date of the last update are shown at the top of this page.

Contact

Please contact us at contact@medivice-cro.com if you have any question about this Privacy Policy or about how we process your data, or if you wish to exercise any of your rights.
