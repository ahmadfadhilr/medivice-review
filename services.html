Clinical Investigation

Pre-market investigations, PMCF studies, registries and post-market surveys — designed to be defensible and run to be finished.

How we run it, and the nine deliverables it produces

Running a device study well is mostly a matter of doing unglamorous things properly and on time: qualifying the right sites, getting ethics and authority submissions right first time, keeping the investigational device supply chain moving, and monitoring closely enough that problems surface while they are still fixable.

Device people, not pharma people

Our project managers and CRAs are device people. They understand implant accountability, device deficiencies and how these differ from adverse events, and why a protocol deviation on a device study can propagate into the technical file.

Indonesia & SE Asia execution

Site qualification and investigator engagement, ethics committee and Kemenkes submissions, hospital and investigator contracting, investigational device import and customs documentation, in-country monitoring and site training — carried out by our own team, not subcontracted.

Standard

Conducted to ISO 14155 and ICH-GCP principles, so the data travels between jurisdictions.

  • Feasibility and site landscape assessment

  • Ethics committee and authority submission
  • Site selection, qualification, initiation
  • Trial agreements and study budgets
  • Project management and monitoring
  • Medical monitoring and safety reporting
  • Device accountability, TMF, CIR/CSR

02

Clinical Evaluation & Medical Writing

The clinical evaluation is where a device's claims meet its evidence. Most reviewer findings originate here.

How we build the argument, and what you receive

We write clinical evaluation plans and reports under EU MDR 2017/745 and MEDDEV 2.7/1 rev 4 conventions. That means a defensible claims matrix traced to the IFU and labelling, a reproducible literature search protocol, appraisal criteria applied consistently rather than retrospectively, and an honest statement of what the evidence does and does not establish.

On equivalence

Equivalence is treated with the scepticism reviewers now apply to it. Where equivalence will not hold, we say so early — and turn the residual gap into a PMCF plan or an investigation rather than a deficiency letter.

Reviewer support

Gap analysis of an existing CER against current expectations, mock review before submission, structured responses to non-conformities and deficiency questions, and audit-day support.

Documents we produce
  • Clinical Evaluation Plan (CEP)
  • Clinical Evaluation Report (CER)
  • Literature search protocol and report
  • Claims and evidence traceability matrix
  • State-of-the-art and benefit–risk analysis
  • Equivalence justification and gap review
  • PMS plan, PMS report and PSUR
  • PMCF plan and PMCF evaluation report
  • SSCP and scientific publications

03

Regulatory Affairs & Market Access

Registration is a documentation problem before it is an administrative one. We work on both ends.

How we handle submissions, and what you receive

For Indonesia we advise on device classification and the applicable Permenkes requirements, assemble and quality-check the registration dossier, and manage submission and follow-up through the Kemenkes electronic registration system. We keep the clinical evidence in the dossier consistent with the clinical evaluation behind it — a mismatch that causes more delay than any other single issue.

Regional reach

Regionally we support ASEAN submissions built on the CSDT structure, and advise on how one evidence package can be sequenced across markets rather than rebuilt for each.

Jurisdictions

Indonesia (Kemenkes / Permenkes) · ASEAN (AMDD & CSDT) · EU (MDR 2017/745) · Singapore (HSA) · Malaysia (MDA) · Thailand · Philippines · Vietnam · China (NMPA bridging)

Our regulatory and clinical teams sit together — the dossier and the evidence are never written apart.

What this covers
  • Device classification and pathway
  • Intended purpose and claims review
  • Regulatory strategy and roadmap
  • Indonesian registration (Izin Edar) dossier
  • Kemenkes e-submission and follow-up
  • Technical documentation gap analysis
  • ASEAN CSDT dossier compilation
  • EU MDR technical file clinical sections
  • Labelling, renewals and notifications
04

Clinical Data Management & Biostatistics

A clean, locked, defensible database is the deliverable that everything else in a study depends on.

How we handle your data, and what you receive

We build and validate electronic data capture systems around the protocol, not the other way round. eCRFs are designed with the analysis in mind, edit checks are specified before go-live rather than patched afterwards, and the data management plan states in advance how every category of discrepancy will be handled.

Data that can be reused

Studies run in Indonesia and Southeast Asia are structured so the resulting dataset can support submissions in other markets — a decision made at protocol and eCRF design stage, not at reporting stage.

Set up on the assumption that someone will one day ask exactly how a value changed, and why.

What this covers
  • EDC selection, build and validation
  • eCRF design and completion guidelines
  • Data Management Plan and edit checks
  • Data review and query management
  • Medical coding and terminology
  • External and safety data reconciliation
  • Quality control, lock and archiving
  • Sample size and Statistical Analysis Plan
  • Statistical analysis, TLFs and reporting
05

Quality, Auditing & Training

Compliance is easier to build in than to retrofit — at a site, in a study, or in an organisation.

How we audit and train, and what you receive

We audit clinical investigations against ISO 14155, the protocol and applicable national requirements, and prepare sites and sponsors for inspection by simulating one. Findings come with a practical corrective action route, not a list of observations to be admired.

Our training sessions are workshop-based and use real documents — protocols, consent forms, monitoring reports, deviation logs — rather than slides alone.

Who we train

Investigators and site study teams · Study coordinators and research nurses · Sponsor clinical and regulatory staff · Ethics committee members · Manufacturers building a first in-house clinical function · License holders taking on post-market obligations

Auditing
  • Clinical investigation and site audits
  • TMF audit, mock inspection
  • Vendor audit, CAPA, SOP framework
Training topics
  • GCP and ISO 14155 in practice
  • Clinical evaluation and PMCF under MDR
  • Monitoring, SDV and safety reporting
  • Delivered in English or Bahasa Indonesia
