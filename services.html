Clinical Investigation

Pre-market clinical investigations, PMCF studies, registries, and post-market surveys — designed to generate robust evidence, withstand regulatory scrutiny, and reach completion.

How we run it, and what you receive

What Good Device Study Execution Looks Like

Running a device study well means getting the unglamorous things right — qualifying the right sites, getting ethics and regulatory submissions right the first time, keeping investigational devices moving, and monitoring closely enough to resolve problems while they are still manageable.

Device people, not pharma people

Our Project Managers and CRAs understand medical device studies. They know implant accountability, device deficiencies and how they differ from adverse events, and how a protocol deviation can affect the clinical evidence and technical documentation.

Indonesia & Southeast Asia execution

Site qualification and investigator engagement, ethics and Kemenkes submissions, hospital and investigator contracting, investigational device import and customs documentation, in-country monitoring, and site training — delivered by our own team, not subcontracted.

Our standard

Conducted in accordance with ISO 14155 and applicable GCP principles, with documentation and data designed to support cross-border regulatory use.

How a Study Runs, from Start-up to Lock

Study start-up is where timelines are often won or lost. We run critical workstreams in parallel, maintain a visible site activation plan, and keep the study moving through conduct and close-out.

Start-up
  • Feasibility & site landscape assessment
  • Clinical Investigation Plan (CIP) & protocol design
  • Ethics committee & regulatory authority submissions
  • Site selection, qualification & initiation
  • Contracts, budgets & essential document collection
  • EDC selection, build & validation
  • Investigator & site training
  • Investigational device import & release
Conduct
  • Dedicated Project Manager & study team
  • Risk-based monitoring & SDV strategy
  • Enrolment & site performance management
  • Medical monitoring & safety reporting
  • Rolling data review & query resolution
  • Protocol deviation management
  • Investigational device accountability
  • Ongoing TMF maintenance
Close-out
  • Database lock & final data review
  • Statistical analysis & reporting
  • Final TMF reconciliation & archiving
  • CIR/CSR writing & dossier integration
  • Handover into post-market obligations
  • Publication support, where required
02

Clinical Evaluation & Medical Writing

The clinical evaluation is where a device’s claims meet its evidence — and where many regulatory findings begin.

How we build the evidence, and what you receive

From Claims to a Defensible Clinical Evaluation

We build clinical evaluations around the claims your device needs to support and the regulatory scrutiny they will face. That means traceability from claims to the IFU and labelling, a reproducible literature search, consistent appraisal criteria, and a clear statement of what the evidence does — and does not — establish.

On equivalence

We approach equivalence with the scrutiny it deserves. Where equivalence cannot be adequately demonstrated, we identify the gap early and determine what evidence is needed to close it — whether through additional literature, clinical investigation, PMCF, or other appropriate clinical data.

Reviewer support

Gap analysis, pre-submission mock review, foreign clinical data justification, responses to Notified Body and regulatory authority questions, and support through regulatory review.

Documents we produce
  • Clinical Evaluation Plan (CEP)
  • Clinical Evaluation Report (CER)
  • Literature search protocol & report
  • Claims & clinical evidence traceability matrix
  • State-of-the-art assessment
  • Benefit–risk assessment
  • Equivalence assessment & gap analysis
  • Foreign clinical data applicability & justification
  • PMS Plan & PMS Report
  • PSUR
  • PMCF Plan & PMCF Evaluation Report
  • SSCP
  • Scientific publications
03

Regulatory Affairs & Market Access

Registration starts with the right evidence and documentation. We work across both — from regulatory strategy to submission and market access.

How we handle submissions, and what you receive

From Regulatory Strategy to Submission

For Indonesia, we advise on device classification and applicable Permenkes requirements, build and quality-check registration dossiers, and manage submission and follow-up through the Kemenkes electronic registration system. We keep the clinical evidence in the dossier aligned with the underlying clinical evaluation — because inconsistencies between the two can create avoidable review questions and delays.

Regional reach

We support ASEAN submissions using the CSDT framework and help sponsors sequence a core evidence package across markets rather than rebuild it for each jurisdiction.

Jurisdictions

Indonesia (Kemenkes / Permenkes) · ASEAN (AMDD & CSDT) · EU (MDR 2017/745) · Singapore (HSA) · Malaysia (MDA) · Thailand · Philippines · Vietnam · China (NMPA bridging)

Our regulatory and clinical teams work together — so the dossier and the evidence behind it are never developed in isolation.

What this covers
  • Device classification & regulatory pathway
  • Intended purpose & claims review
  • Regulatory strategy & roadmap
  • Indonesian registration (Izin Edar) dossier
  • Kemenkes e-submission & follow-up
  • Technical documentation gap analysis
  • ASEAN CSDT dossier compilation
  • EU MDR clinical sections of technical documentation
  • Labelling, renewals & regulatory notifications
04

Clinical Data Management & Biostatistics

A clean, locked, and defensible database is the foundation of every analysis, conclusion, and clinical claim that follows.

How we build the data, and what you receive

Data Designed for Analysis and Reuse

We build and validate electronic data capture systems around the protocol — not the other way around. eCRFs are designed with the analysis in mind, edit checks are specified before go-live rather than patched afterwards, and the Data Management Plan defines in advance how each category of discrepancy will be handled.

Data that can be reused

Studies conducted in Indonesia and Southeast Asia are structured so the resulting dataset can support submissions in other markets — a decision made at the protocol and eCRF design stage, not at reporting.

Built for the questions a reviewer will ask: what changed, when, and why.

What this covers
  • EDC selection, build & validation
  • eCRF design & completion guidelines
  • Data Management Plan & edit checks
  • Data review & query management
  • Medical coding & terminology
  • External & safety data reconciliation
  • Quality control, database lock & archiving
  • Sample size calculation & Statistical Analysis Plan
  • Statistical analysis, TLFs & reporting
05

Quality, Auditing & Training

Compliance is easier to build in than to retrofit — at the site, within the study, or across the organisation.

How we audit and train, and what you receive

Practical Compliance, Built to Last

We audit clinical investigations against ISO 14155, the protocol, and applicable national requirements, and prepare sites and sponsors for inspection by simulating the scrutiny they may face. Findings come with a practical corrective action path — not a list of observations to be admired.

Our training is workshop-based and built around real study documents — protocols, informed consent forms, monitoring reports, deviation logs, and other working materials — rather than slides alone.

Who we train

Investigators & site study teams · Study coordinators & research nurses · Sponsor clinical & regulatory teams · Ethics committee members · Manufacturers building an in-house clinical function · License holders managing post-market obligations

Auditing
  • Clinical investigation & site audits
  • TMF audits & mock inspections
  • Vendor audits & qualification
  • CAPA assessment & follow-up
  • SOP framework review
Training topics
  • GCP & ISO 14155 in practice
  • Clinical evaluation & PMCF under EU MDR
  • Monitoring, SDV & safety reporting
  • Protocol deviations & risk management
  • Essential documents & TMF
  • Clinical investigation readiness
