Your Device, From Clinical Investigation to Post-Market

Evidence Obligations Do Not Stop at Market Entry

A medical device does not stop generating evidence obligations once it reaches the market. We support manufacturers at any point in the lifecycle — or across the entire continuum.

  1. PHASE 01

    Clinical Strategy

    • Claims & intended use
    • Clinical evidence gap analysis
    • Regulatory pathway
    • Clinical development plan
  2. PHASE 02

    Study & Evidence Design

    • Protocol & synopsis
    • Endpoints & sample size
    • Risk–benefit rationale
    • Clinical evidence strategy
  3. PHASE 03

    Study Start-Up

    • Site selection & feasibility
    • Ethics & regulatory submissions
    • Contracts & budgets
    • EDC build & validation
  4. PHASE 04

    Study Conduct & Monitoring

    • Project management
    • On-site & remote monitoring
    • Safety reporting
    • Data management
  5. PHASE 05

    Analysis, Reporting & Submission

    • Statistical analysis
    • Clinical Investigation Report (CIR) / Clinical Study Report (CSR)
    • CER & technical documentation
    • Regulatory submission dossier
  6. PHASE 06

    Post-Market Evidence

    • PMS & PMCF strategy
    • PMCF studies & surveys
    • Registries & real-world evidence
    • PSUR & CER updates

The two questions we hear most often are: “Is this enough clinical evidence?” and “What happens if it isn’t?” Both can be answered before the money is spent.
